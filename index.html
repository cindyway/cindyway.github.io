About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in the Department of Computer Science at City University of Hong Kong, under the supervision of Prof. WONG Hau San. I am fully funded by the Postgraduate Studentship based on academic excellence.

My research lies at the cutting-edge intersection of Deep Learning and Bioinformatics (AI4Bio). I specialize in designing advanced Transformer architectures and Contrastive Learning frameworks to decode cellular spatial heterogeneity, single-cell modalities alignment, and downstream clinical/drug response modeling.
